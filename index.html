Why one-piece only

“A dress is the only garment that has to get shoulder, waist and hip right at once. That is a whole craft. We did not want a second one.”

Inês Cardoso, pattern cutter & founder

Inside the atelier
  1. 01

    Measured standing, in shoes

    Bust, waist, hip, shoulder, nape-to-floor. Eight minutes with a tape and a pencil, in the room, before anything is cut.

  2. 02

    Hemmed to your height

    Every dress leaves at the length you asked for, pinned on you. No standard drop, no guessing from a size chart.

  3. 03

    A year of alterations

    Bodies change. Bring it back within twelve months and we take it in, let it out, or shorten the strap. No charge.

  4. 04

    Repairs for as long as it lasts

    A snagged seam, a lost button, a worn underarm. Post it to Cedofeita and we mend it — you cover the postage back.
